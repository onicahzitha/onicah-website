About THREAD

Fashion that is accessible, expressive and yours.

THREAD is a contemporary fashion and streetwear retail store aimed at young people who use fashion as a form of personal expression. The store offers modern clothing inspired by current trends, streetwear and different fashion aesthetics.


Our collection includes clothing for both men and women. We design each piece to blend comfort, confidence, and everyday style.
