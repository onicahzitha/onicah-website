THREAD – Content Research and Project Documentation

Part One – Content Research

Organisation Background

THREAD is a proposed contemporary fashion and streetwear retail store aimed at teenagers and young adults. The brand focuses on fashion as a form of personal expression, offering clothing and accessories influenced by modern trends, streetwear and different fashion aesthetics.

The website combines an online retail experience with fashion inspiration. The original concept was to allow visitors to browse products while also discovering outfit ideas, collections and fashion-related content.

During the development of Part Two, the concept was developed into a functional clothing-store website. The implemented website focuses primarily on the online shopping experience, with sections for the home page, products, shopping cart, About Us and Contact.

Target Audience Research

The main target audience for THREAD is teenagers, young adults, fashion-conscious consumers and streetwear enthusiasts. This audience is generally interested in current fashion trends, personal style and visually appealing content.

The website therefore uses strong visual presentation, straightforward navigation, product photography and concise product information. The dark colour scheme and streetwear-inspired presentation were also selected to create a modern appearance suitable for the intended audience.

Collection Research

The original content research proposed several collections, including New Arrivals, Streetwear, Y2K, Essentials and Seasonal Collections.

During Part Two, the website moved towards a simpler product-category structure. Instead of implementing the proposed collections as separate navigation pages, products are currently organised according to clothing categories such as Tops, Outerwear and Bottoms.

The website also includes a More Products section, which provides space for additional products and future product expansion.

Lookbook Content

The original research proposed a Lookbook section containing outfit inspiration and editorial-style fashion photography.

This concept was not implemented as a separate Lookbook page in the current Part Two version. The visual fashion focus was instead incorporated into the product presentation through product photography and the overall visual design of the website.

A Lookbook could be added as a future development to expand the website’s fashion inspiration content.

Style Journal

The original research proposed a Style Journal containing fashion-related articles, styling guides, trend information and outfit inspiration.

The Style Journal has not been implemented as a separate section in the current Part Two version. The current website prioritises the core retail functionality, including product browsing, shopping-cart functionality, brand information and customer contact.

A Style Journal could be added in a future version to provide additional editorial content.

Visual Content Research

Visual content is an important part of THREAD’s website because fashion relies heavily on photography and visual presentation.

Part Two implemented product photography throughout the Shop and More Products sections. A THREAD logo image is also used within the About section and as part of the visual branding.

The homepage uses a large hero area with a THREAD-branded background image. The image is combined with a dark overlay to ensure that the text remains visible while maintaining the streetwear aesthetic.

Images used on the website are either created for the project or sourced for appropriate use. Copyright and licensing requirements should be considered when selecting and using external images.

Brand Content

THREAD’s brand identity focuses on individuality, modern fashion and youth culture. The website therefore uses a confident, simple and approachable tone.

The implemented tagline is:

“Your Style. Your Era.”

This tagline is displayed in the website header, homepage and footer and is used as an important part of the brand identity.

The homepage also uses the phrase:

“Curated for the way you move.”

The website avoids overly formal language and instead uses short, direct wording that suits the intended young audience.

Visual Identity

Part Two established a more defined visual identity for THREAD.

The primary colours used in the website include:

* Black
* Charcoal
* White
* Light grey
* Mustard yellow
* Bright yellow

Black is used as the primary background colour, while charcoal is used for product cards and other content areas. White and light grey are used for readable text, while yellow and mustard are used for highlights, buttons and important brand elements.

The website uses Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif as its main font family. The design uses uppercase navigation labels, letter spacing and bold headings to create a clean streetwear-inspired appearance.

Home Page

The homepage introduces visitors to the THREAD brand.

The hero section contains:

* THREAD branding
* The tagline “Your Style. Your Era.”
* A welcome message
* The phrase “Curated for the way you move.”
* A large THREAD background image

The hero section is designed to immediately communicate the identity of the store and create a strong first impression.

Shop Section

The Shop section is the main product area of the website.

Products are displayed using individual product cards containing:

* Product image
* Product name
* Gender/clothing type
* Product category
* Price
* Add to Cart button

The product layout uses a responsive grid, allowing multiple products to be displayed across the page on larger screens and adjusting to a single-column layout on smaller screens.

Shopping Cart

Part Two introduces shopping-cart functionality.

Users can add products to the cart using the Add to Cart buttons. The cart section displays:

* Cart items
* Total price
* Clear Cart button
* Checkout button

This functionality changes the website from a static product showcase into a basic interactive online-store prototype.

About Us Section

The About Us section provides information about the THREAD brand.

The implemented content describes THREAD as a contemporary fashion and streetwear retail store aimed at young people who use fashion as a form of personal expression.

It also explains that THREAD was established around the idea that fashion should be accessible, expressive and reflective of individual identity.

The section also states that the collection includes clothing for both men and women and focuses on comfort, confidence and everyday style.

A Shop Now button provides a call-to-action that directs visitors to the Shop section.

Contact Section

The Contact section provides customers with basic contact information and a contact form.

The implemented contact information includes:

* Email: info@threadstore.com
* Phone: 012 345 6789

The contact form collects:

* Name
* Email
* Message

The form fields include basic HTML validation for the name and email fields.

Responsive Design

Part Two includes responsive CSS to improve the website’s usability on smaller screens.

A media query is used for screens below 700px. On smaller screens:

* The header changes to a vertical layout.
* Navigation spacing is reduced.
* Product cards are displayed in a single-column layout.
* Section headings are adjusted for smaller screens.

This allows the website to adapt to different screen sizes instead of being designed only for desktop viewing.

Research Influence on the Website

The content research influenced the final website by establishing THREAD as a youth-focused contemporary fashion and streetwear brand.

The research influenced the use of:

* Fashion-focused product photography
* A strong visual identity
* Simple product categories
* Concise product descriptions
* Clear navigation
* Youth-oriented brand language
* A shopping-cart feature
* Responsive design

The original research also identified a Lookbook and Style Journal as possible features. These were not implemented in the current Part Two version, allowing the project to focus on the core online-store experience.
